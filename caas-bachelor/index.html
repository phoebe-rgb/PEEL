Le Bouveret campus image — Lake Geneva
Years 1–2

Le Bouveret

Our primary campus houses 17 of our 18 kitchens and three of our four student training restaurants. Set on the shores of Lake Geneva near the French border, it offers breathtaking views of the Swiss Riviera and direct access to Switzerland's rich agricultural regions. The Apicius building is the training hub — 18 specialized kitchens supporting technical precision across culinary arts, pastry, chocolate, and food R&D.

Brig campus image — Valais / the Alps
Year 3

Brig

In your final year you move to Brig, in the Valais region at the foot of the Alps, to focus on business, entrepreneurship, and strategic restaurant management. It's the setting where you bring three years of craft and theory together — ready to manage an establishment or launch your own.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What degree will I graduate with?

Two globally recognized Bachelor's degrees: a Bachelor of International Business in your chosen pathway from Culinary Arts Academy Switzerland, and a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Culinary Arts from the University of Derby, UK.

How long is the program and can I exit early?

It's a three-year program including two 6-month internships. The multi-exit structure lets you complete one, two, or three years and earn a recognized Swiss award at each stage — a Swiss Grand Diploma after Year 1 and a Swiss Higher Diploma after Year 2.

When can I start?

Culinary Arts intakes are in January, April, July and October. Pastry, Bakery & Chocolate Arts intakes are in January and July.

Where will I study?

Years 1 and 2 are at our Le Bouveret campus on Lake Geneva — home to 18 state-of-the-art kitchens and our student-run restaurants. Year 3 is at our Brig campus in the Valais, focused on business and entrepreneurship.

What are the entry requirements?

A recommended minimum age of 18, completed secondary education (IB, Maturité, Abitur, French Bac, 2 A-levels, high school diploma or equivalent), and IELTS academic 5.5 or equivalent (Duolingo 95). Direct-entry, credit-transfer and prior-learning (RPL + RPEL) routes may also be available.

Will I get real industry experience?

Yes — 1,100 practical kitchen hours, two 6-month internships in Switzerland or abroad, and hands-on experience operating a 50-seat restaurant open to the public.
