Program overview

BACHELOR of ARTS in CULINARY ARTS or PASTRY ARTS

Refine your craft over an immersive three-year program that blends hands-on training with entrepreneurial insight. Choose your pathway — Culinary Arts, or Pastry, Bakery & Chocolate Arts — and work toward a bachelor's degree through a flexible, multi-exit structure that awards a Swiss Diploma at the end of each year.

Le Bouveret campus image — Lake Geneva
Years 1–2

Le Bouveret

Our primary campus houses 17 of our 18 kitchens and three of our four student training restaurants. Set on the shores of Lake Geneva near the French border, it offers breathtaking views of the Swiss Riviera and direct access to Switzerland's rich agricultural regions. Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What degree will I graduate with?

Two globally recognized Bachelor's degrees: a Bachelor of International Business in your chosen pathway from Culinary Arts Academy Switzerland, and a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Culinary Arts from the University of Derby, UK — worth 360 UK credits / 180 ECTS.

How long is the Bachelor, and can I exit early?

It's a three-year program including two 6-month internships. The flexible, multi-exit structure lets you complete one, two, or three years and earn a recognized Swiss award at each stage — a Swiss Grand Diploma after Year 1 and a Swiss Higher Diploma after Year 2 — before your full Bachelor's degree in Year 3.

When can I start?

Culinary Arts intakes are in January, April, July and October. Pastry, Bakery & Chocolate Arts intakes are in January and July. The next intake is January 2027.

Are the degrees internationally recognized?

Yes. Your CAAS degree is awarded alongside a University of Derby (UK) degree and is respected by employers across the globe. Studying at Switzerland's #1-ranked culinary school (QS World University Rankings 2026) gives your qualification lasting international value.

What are the entry requirements for international students?

A recommended minimum age of 18, completed secondary education (IB, IBCP, Maturité, Abitur, French Bac, 2 A-levels, high school diploma or equivalent), and English proficiency of IELTS academic 5.5 or equivalent (Duolingo 95). Direct-entry, credit-transfer (IBCP, IBDP, BTEC) and prior-learning (RPL + RPEL) routes may also be available.

Do I need to arrange accommodation, meals, and my visa myself?

No. Our programs are all-inclusive — covering tuition, accommodation, and meals — and our admissions team supports you through the entire visa process, guiding you every step of the way from acceptance to arrival. We recommend applying early to allow 8–12 weeks for the visa.

Are scholarships and payment plans available?

Yes. We offer merit-based scholarships, flexible payment plans, and education-loan options. Our admissions team will walk you through the options that fit your situation.

Will I gain real, paid work experience?

Yes — the Bachelor includes two 6-month paid internships in Switzerland or abroad, plus career coaching, CV support, and access to an international recruitment forum with 100+ global employers.

Is campus life safe and welcoming for international students?

Absolutely. Switzerland is among the top 10 safest countries in the world, and our campuses bring together 100+ nationalities with dedicated international student services and on-site support throughout your studies.
