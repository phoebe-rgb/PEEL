Why Study in Switzerland

A springboard for international careers

If you have never been to Switzerland, here is what it is: a small, safe, central European country with a rich culinary heritage, world-famous chefs, and a vocational training tradition that employers everywhere recognize. The Campuses

Two campuses, one world-class culinary journey

Study on the shores of Lake Geneva in Le Bouveret or in the sunny Rhône Valley in Brig. Both campuses pair stunning Swiss settings with 18 state-of-the-art training kitchens and a training restaurant open to the public, giving you the perfect place to master your craft and launch a global culinary career.

Le Bouveret

A lakeside resort on Lake Geneva, home to the Apicius culinary center and the Mosimann Collection, with state-of-the-art kitchens and highly experienced chefs.

Brig

Nestled at the foot of the Alps in the Valais region, with modern facilities, inspiring spaces and the student-run Restaurant Alpina in town.
