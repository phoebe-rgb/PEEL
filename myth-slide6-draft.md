Hi @Lucie, adding what we did before @Long joined — grouped by workstream for slide 6, no months attached:

**Negative keywords — ongoing from day one**
• Built an automated review: search terms pulled every 2 days, scored against 9 irrelevant-traffic categories, full flagged list to a Sheet with a short summary in Slack
• The categories: AI video tools, animation software/DIY, named competitor studios, stop motion, non-London UK cities, VFX/post-production, freelancer signals, explainer videos, kids/anime
• Flagged volume fell from 250–300 terms a run at launch to 20–50 a run by July — the list was doing its job. The biggest drains were competitor studio names (90+ terms a run: Pixar, Aardman, Blue Zoo, Escape Studios) and AI tools (higgsfield 117 impressions, pollo ai 70, popvid 51)

**Job enquiries and other non-buyer traffic — tackled four ways**
• Freelancer-signal negatives: animator for hire, showreel, 2d animators, uk animation directory
• Negative in-market audiences applied to both live campaigns
• Zap filtered to pass only leads carrying UTMs, so contact-form job applications and agency pitches stopped clogging the paid funnel in Pipedrive
• Explainer ad group paused at the client's request

**Keyword analysis**
• Found the generic animation company / studio / agency group driving ~70% of both spend and leads, while 2D and 3D had spent ~£600 with no conversions
• Turned Brand off after a lead came through it — nobody was bidding on Myth's name, so we were paying for a conversion we would have had for free. Also decided against competitor brand terms: more expensive, lower conversion rate
• Isolated the AI / hybrid theme when it started pulling broad AI-tool traffic and the client confirmed AI wasn't their focus
• Rebuilt the keyword set around buyer language after James' feedback that the terms read as industry jargon, plus UK volume research on Broadcast & Title Sequences for the positioning push

**Restructure**
• Root-caused the underspend: one campaign on a shared £70/day cap, Animation Company taking ~67% of it while Fast / Expedited received £1.56 in 18 days — the theme had never had the budget to prove itself either way
• Split into separate campaigns with their own daily budgets so every theme got a real test, and staged the scale-up to land the full cycle budget

**Conversion events and tracking**
• Fixed Google Ads conversion tracking and cleared the test leads out of Pipedrive
• Set conversion value to £2,500 on the lead form (£50k average deal × 5% paid win rate), counting one per click on a 30-day window
• Traced the gap between GA conversions and CRM leads to two separate form flows: the success event only confirmed the tracking API had responded, not that the lead reached the CRM
• Added landing-page capture in Zapier so leads arriving without UTMs could still be sourced

**Measurement**
• Built the Looker Studio dashboard, with search terms and lead status by theme — cold / warm / hot alongside cost and lead counts
• Worked around Funnel's missing search-term dimension with a daily Google Ads export, and fixed the sync lag that was understating the daily numbers by ~40%
• Completed Google advertiser verification so the logo showed in the ads

One to add to **Key learnings**: job enquiries were the hardest thing to filter. The same keywords that bring real briefs also bring people looking for work, so no negative list removes them completely without cutting into real demand too.
